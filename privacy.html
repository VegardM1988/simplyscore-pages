Privacy Policy - SimplyScore

Last updated: October 31, 2026

SimplyScore is a standalone Apple Watch application developed by Vegard Markussen.

SimplyScore is designed to work without collecting or sharing personal information.

1. Data Collection

SimplyScore does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. No account or registration is required.

2. Local Data Storage

Game scores and Undo information are stored locally on your Apple Watch. This data is not transmitted to the developer or external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

SimplyScore does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or tracking technologies.

4. Internet Connection

SimplyScore works offline and does not require an internet connection.

5. Children's Privacy

SimplyScore does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, please visit the SimplyScore Support page.
