Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an iPhone to use SimplyScore?

No. SimplyScore works independently on Apple Watch.

Does SimplyScore require internet access?

No. The app works entirely offline.

Will my score be saved?

Yes. SimplyScore automatically saves the current score locally on your Apple Watch.

How do I correct a mistake?

Tap Undo to reverse your last scoring action.

How do I start a new game?

Tap New Game and confirm to reset the score.
